NYC municipal labor contracts

Every agreement the New York City Office of Labor Relations (OLR) lists on its Recent Agreements page, plus five published elsewhere for the city's firefighters, fire officers, public hospital nurses and City University of New York (CUNY) faculty. Many are scanned image PDFs whose text isn't selectable, so this site extracts the text of every page, using optical character recognition (OCR) where needed, to make each agreement searchable, tagged by topic and quotable with a stable link to the source clause. Scanned text contains transcription mistakes: roughly 500 have been hand-corrected, and the documents with the most remaining errors are marked some OCR errors. For a few agreements, including the Committee of Interns and Residents contract and the Professional Staff Congress (PSC) 2023-2027 memorandum of agreement, the text comes instead from a cleaner copy the union publishes. Every document links to its source PDF. Some agreements in force have not been published at all: see known missing documents. Coverage details.
