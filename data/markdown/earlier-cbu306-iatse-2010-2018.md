MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT entered into this lo day of~2 01-4', by
and between the undersigned International Alliance of Theatrical Stage Employees, Local
~06 ("IA TSE" or the "Union"); and the City of New York (!he "City").
WHEREAS, the undersigned parties desire to enter into a collective bargaining
agreement, including this 2010-2018 IATSE MOA and the agreement successor to the one
terminating on October 14, 2010 ("Successor Separate Unit Agreement") to cover the
employees represented by the Union ("Employees");
WHEREAS, the undersigned parties intend by this 2010-2018 IATSE MOA to cover all
economic and non-economic matters and to incorporate the terms of this 2010-2018
IATSE MOA into the Successor Separate Unit Agreement, covering the period from
October 15, 2010 through February 14, 2018;
WHEREAS, the undersigned parties intend by this 2010-2018 /ATSE MOA to continue
all of the same terms and conditions specified in the Separate Unit Agreement, including
applicable side letters, terminating on October 14, 2010, except as modified or amended
below,
NOW, THEREFORE, it is jointly agreed as follows:
1. The term of the Agreement shall be from October 15, 2010 through February 14,
2018.
2. General Wage Increases
a.
i. Effective April 15, 2013, Employees shall receive a general increase of 1.00%.
ii. Effective April 15, 2014, Employees shall receive a general increase of 1.00%.
iii. Effective April 15, 2015, Employees shall receive a general increase of 1.50%.
iv. Effective April 15, 2016, Employees shall receive a general increase of 2.50%.
v. Effective April 15, 2017, Employees shall receive a general increase of3.00%.
vi. Effective October 15, 2017, Employees shall receive a general increase of0.56%
b. Part-time per annum, per session, hourly paid and part-time per diem Employees
(including seasonal appointees) and Employees whose normal work year is less than a
full calendar year shall receive the increases provided in Section 2a on the basis of
computations heretofore utilized by the parties for all such Employees.

c. The general increases provided for in Section 2a above shall be based upon the

3. Longevity Increment
Effective April 15, 2012, the longevity increment identified in Article III,
Section 8 of the Collective Bargaining Agreement shall be amended to include the
following:
Employees with 20 years or more of "City" service in pay status who are
not already eligible for a longevity differential or service increment
established by a salary review or equity panel shall receive a total
longevity increment of $3,344.
Employees with 25 years or more of "City" service in pay status who are
not already eligible for a longevity differential or service increment
established by a salary review or equity panel shall receive a total
longevity increment of$3,704.
4. Additions to Gross
Effective April 15, 2017 the general increase provided for in Section 2a. v
above shall be applied to "additions to gross." "Additions to gross" shall be
defined to include uniform allowances, equipment allowances, transportation
allowances, uniform maintenance allowance, assignment differentials, service
increments, longevity differentials, advancement increases, assignment (level)
increases, and experience, certification, educational, license, evening, or night
shift differentials.

time employees, shall be payable as soon as practicable upon ratification of the
Agreement to those employees who are on payroll as of the date of ratification.
The lump sum cash payment shall be pensionable, consistent with applicable law.

## Page 4

ratification of the Agreement unless the parties have mutually agreed to extend
the deadline. Any claim that either party has of enforcement of a mutually
agreed upon savings proposal shall be submitted to an expedited arbitration panel
with the assistance of the Office of Collective Bargaining. The expedited
arbitration panel shall not be used to decide the substance, merit or value of either
of the parties' specific savings proposals. The final general wage increase in this
Agreement as stated in Section 2a.vi shall not be paid unless and until these
discussions are completed by the parties or unless the parties mutually agree to
extend the deadline.
8. Prohibition of Further Economic Demands
Except as provided for in Section 7, no party to this agreement shall make
additional economic demands during the term of the 2010-2018 IATSE MOA or
during the negotiations for the Successor Unit Agreement.
9. Labor-Management Training Committee
The parties agree to form a labor-management committee consisting of
representatives of the Union, the City Office of Labor Relations, and employing
agencies to discuss mutually agreeable training opportunities for bargaining unit
members, particularly as it relates to technological advances in audio-visual
equipment.
10. Health Savings and Welfare Fund Contributions
The May 5, 2014 Letter Agreement regarding health savings and welfare fund
contributions between the City of New York and the Municipal Labor Committee,
will be attached as an Appendix, and is deemed to be part of this 2010-2018
IATSEMOA
11. The final general wage increase in this Agreement as specified in Section 2a.vi
shall not be paid unless and until there is a signed separate unit agreement.
12. This Memorandum of Agreement is subject to union ratification.
